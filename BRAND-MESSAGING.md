## Design approach
Editorial architecture: structural beams, columns, support, and connection rather than literal bridge imagery. Original architectural study provided as a local website asset `assets/structural-study.webp` with editable SVG source in `assets/structural-study.svg`.

## Voice
Understated, consultative, technically credible. Emphasize joined-up security planning, implementation, and long-term outcomes rather than individual equipment sales.
